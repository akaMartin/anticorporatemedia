# Noncorporate Media

## Purpose 

Noncorporate Media is a social network for keeping up with friends and acquaintances, built so that the networks interests never diverge from those of itsusers.

Existing social media sites drifted toward capturing user time and attention because investor and revenue pressure rewarded it, at the expense of the user experience. Inshitification does not need to be inevitable. 

Noncorporate Media (noncorporatemedia.com) is a social network for keeping up with friends and acquaintances, built so that its interests never diverge from those of its users.s This project rejects that incentive, not the premise of social media or technology itself. We want to foster connection and communication with people in the real world, not farm attention and personal information for ad revenue.

## Core Principles

These principles define how the project is designed to work, and all design decisions will be tested against them. 

1. **Mutual friendship only.** Social media should encourage mutual interaction and consent, not incentivize parasocial relationships or broadcast media. 
2. **Content is for keeping up with friends**, not capturing user attention. We only display content your friends post or share, in reverse chronological order. No suggested posts, no algorithmic attention seeking.
3. **Good technology demands less of us, not more.** Technology should allow us to live more independently of technology. Things like infinite scroll designed to keep you on a site longer are not good technologies, they are tools of extraction. 
4. **Our incentives are your incentives.** We just want an app where we can keep up with our friends who we don't see every day. There's no venture funding to pay back and no IPO to chase; we have day jobs and build this part time. If we ever need money for server and maintenance costs, we'll ask for donations or show a few untargeted ads, never ones based on your data, and publish every dollar in and out on the site.
5. **Open source everything.** In keeping with that transparency, the code, the infrastructure and our decisions are all public. We have nothing to hide, and you stay in control of your own data.

We're not trying to be too heavy handed about this. We  want people to have fun on social media again.

## AI Use Policy

The purpose of technology is to make being human easier. We treat AI as we would any other technology--something to augment or automate things that suck to do so that we can spend more time doing the things we want to spend our time doing. 

- The purpose of social media is to connect with other humans
- The purpose of technology is to make that connection easier by reducing bariers (geographic, lingual, temporal, etc.)
- We will use technology to automate things like writing and deploying software, as well as some product management, because we don't believe these things are inherent to human nature. 
- However, ideation, design, and decision making will remain largely human
- Interacting with others should be a human thing, which is kind of the whole point of fostering connection. If you're automating human connection, why are you even trying to make that connection in the first place? 
- We're automating the wires. Let's keep what's going over those wires human.
